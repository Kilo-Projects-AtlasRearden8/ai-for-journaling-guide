# AI for Journaling — A Beginner's Manual

Welcome. This guide is for anyone who wants to use AI as a journaling companion — without sacrificing privacy, simplicity, or your own voice.

Whether you're picking up a pen (or keyboard) for the first time or you've been journaling for years, AI can gently support your practice with thoughtful prompts, pattern recognition, and low-effort organization. We keep it warm, low-pressure, and firmly under your control.

> *This is a beginner-friendly manual created by Asdale Dynamics, LLC. We explain AI like a friend would: plain English, step by step, never condescending.*

## Who This Is For

- **Writers and note-takers** who want structure without the friction
- **Healthcare workers and caregivers** seeking a reflective outlet (educational only — not medical advice)
- **Anyone with ADHD or low energy** who benefits from gentle, low-barrier systems
- **Privacy-conscious users** who want local-first or self-hosted tools
- **Beginners** who find AI intimidating

## A Kind, Low-Pressure Approach

We don't aim for perfection. AI journaling is about:

- **Starting small** — even one sentence a day counts
- **Being kind to yourself** — there are no "wrong" entries
- **Privacy first** — your thoughts belong to you
- **Local-first when possible** — tools you control, not platforms you rent

## How This Site Works

This manual is a static site built from Markdown source files. You can:

- Browse the [online site](site/index.html) (if published)
- Build it locally with `python3 build.py`
- Print any page or template for offline journaling
- Download and adapt the templates for your own use

## Quick Start in 60 Seconds

1. **Choose a tool** — pick an AI assistant you trust (see [Getting Started](getting-started.html) for free/local options)
2. **Start a journal entry** — open a blank document or printed page
3. **Ask AI for a prompt** — "What's one thing worth reflecting on today?"
4. **Write freely** — respond in your own words, no editing needed
5. **Save it** — keep entries in a folder you control
6. **Come back tomorrow** — consistency beats intensity
