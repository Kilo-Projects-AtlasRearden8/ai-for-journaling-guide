# Reflection Prompts — Questions That Matter

The magic of AI journaling isn't in replacing your thoughts — it's in asking
the *right* questions at the right time. This page collects gentle, open-ended
prompts you can use daily, weekly, and monthly.

## How to Use These Prompts

- **Copy one** into your journal or AI tool
- **Don't overthink** the response — write freely for 2–5 minutes
- **Follow up** with "Tell me more about that" or "What resonates here?"
- **Bookmark** the ones that click for you

## Daily Reflection Prompts

These are for morning intention-setting or evening wind-down. Pick one:

1. "What's one thing I'm carrying today — a thought, feeling, or worry?"
2. "If today had a color, what would it be — and why?"
3. "What's something small I could do for myself today?"
4. "What's one interaction today that stayed with me — and why?"
5. "If I could give yesterday-me one piece of advice, what would it be?"
6. "What's one thing I'm grateful for that I often overlook?"
7. "What would it feel like to set down one thing I'm holding too tightly?"
8. "What does my body need right now — really?"

## Weekly Reflection Prompts

Use these on Sunday evening or Monday morning:

1. "What were three moments this week that felt 'like me'?"
2. "When did I feel most alive this week? What was happening?"
3. "Is there a pattern in what drained me vs. what gave me energy?"
4. "What's one assumption I'm holding that might no longer serve me?"
5. "If I could celebrate one small win from this week, what would it be?"
6. "What's one thing I'm curious about for next week?"
7. "What story am I telling myself about this week — and is it accurate?"

## Monthly Reflection Prompts

1. "Looking back, what themes kept showing up this month?"
2. "What's one thing I've learned about myself this month?"
3. "If I could protect one feeling or habit into next month, what would it be?"
4. "What's one boundary I need to set or reinforce?"
5. "What does 'enough' feel like for me right now?"

## AI-Generated Prompts: A Sample Workflow

Instead of using a fixed list, let AI tailor prompts to your entries:

```
User: "Yesterday I felt frustrated during a meeting — I couldn't
       get my point across and left feeling unseen."

AI:    "What part of that interaction do you think you'll replay
       in your mind tonight? And what might you want to say to
       yourself about it tomorrow morning?"
```

## Prompts for Specific Goals

### Processing a Difficult Emotion
- "What is this emotion trying to tell me?"
- "Where do I feel it in my body?"
- "What would compassion say to this version of me?"

### Sparking Creativity
- "What's a half-forgotten idea I keep circling back to?"
- "If I had no constraints, what would I create?"
- "What story from my life deserves to be told?"

### Building Habits
- "What's one tiny step I could take tomorrow?"
- "What would 'good enough' look like?"
- "What's getting in the way of starting?"

## Prompt Generator Template

You can ask AI to generate custom prompts for you:

> "You're a gentle journaling companion. I journal about [TOPIC — e.g.,
> stress at work, creative blocks, relationships]. Give me three
> warm, open-ended prompts for today — nothing clinical, no advice, just
> questions that invite curiosity."
