Printable Journal Templates

Sometimes paper is better. A printed page doesn't need charging, won't
distract you with notifications, and lets you write without a screen between
you and your thoughts.

This page collects printable templates you can download, print, and use
immediately.

Available Templates

Template Best For Pages
Daily Journal Morning or evening check-ins 1 page
Mood Tracker Tracking energy & mood over a week 1 page
Weekly Review Sunday reflection & planning 1 page
Reflection Cards Prompts you can cut out & draw from 4 cards/page
Insight Worksheet Monthly pattern review 1 page

How to Use These Templates

  1. Open any .html file in the templates/ folder
  2. Print using your browser (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P)
  3. Fill in by hand — pen or pencil
  4. File in a binder, folder, or notebook

Print Settings

For best results:

  • Paper: US Letter (8.5" × 11") or A4
  • Margins: None or minimal (0.5")
  • Orientation: Portrait
  • Color: Optional — templates print fine in black & white

Daily Journal Template

A single page for one day:

  • Date & day of week
  • Mood check-in (word or 1–5 scale)
  • "Today I'm grateful for..."
  • "One thing I want to remember..."
  • Free-writing space (lined)
  • Evening reflection: "How did today go?"

Mood Tracker Template

One week per page:

  • 7 columns (Mon–Sun)
  • Energy level (1–5 or word)
  • Mood word
  • One event tied to the mood
  • Notes section at bottom

Weekly Review Template

For Sunday evenings:

  • Wins from this week
  • Challenges I faced
  • What drained my energy
  • What gave me energy
  • One thing to carry into next week
  • One thing to let go of

Reflection Cards

Printable cards with gentle prompts. Cut them out, fold them, and draw one
when you're stuck:

  • "What's one thing I'm carrying today?"
  • "What would my best self do right now?"
  • "What am I avoiding — and why?"
  • "What does 'enough' look like today?"
  • Plus 12 more

Insight Worksheet

For monthly pattern review:

  • Recurring themes I noticed
  • Mood trends (up, down, steady)
  • One thing I learned about myself
  • One boundary I need
  • One experiment for next month

Digital Alternative

If you prefer typing, you can also:

  • Open the template HTML in your browser and type directly
  • Save as PDF from your browser
  • Copy the template into your preferred note-taking app

All templates are plain HTML/CSS — no special software required.
